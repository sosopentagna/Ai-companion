🍃 A grounding exercise

If you feel overwhelmed, try gently bringing your attention to the present moment. There is no need to force yourself to feel calm.

  1. Name five things you can see.
  2. Notice four things you can physically feel.
  3. Identify three sounds you can hear.
  4. Notice two scents, if any are present.
  5. Name one taste or one thing you appreciate in this moment.

🌬️ Gentle breathing break

Let your breathing remain comfortable. If focusing on breathing feels uncomfortable, skip this exercise and try noticing objects around you.
