A little space to talk, reflect, and take care of yourself.

💜 Companion personality

Your companion's guiding personality is gentle, patient, curious, compassionate, and encouraging. It listens without judgment, respects your boundaries, and helps you think through difficult feelings without telling you what you must feel.

This adds your chosen style to the next message you send. It does not change the AI model or its permanent backend settings.

📖 Guided journaling

Write whatever is on your mind. You do not need to make it perfect.

Your writing stays in this box until you choose to discuss it. This add-on does not save journal entries permanently.

🍃 A grounding exercise

If you feel overwhelmed, try gently bringing your attention to the present moment. There is no need to force yourself to feel calm.

  1. Name five things you can see.
  2. Notice four things you can physically feel.
  3. Identify three sounds you can hear.
  4. Notice two scents, if any are present.
  5. Name one taste or one thing you appreciate in this moment.

🌬️ Gentle breathing break

Let your breathing remain comfortable. If focusing on breathing feels uncomfortable, skip this exercise and try noticing objects around you.
